          Terrain
          
              
    
          
      
          The same capture as geometry — a dense point cloud and a
        surface you can take into Blender, QGIS or Rhino and draw from. Nothing is
        re-captured or re-solved.
          
      
      
          Not for a scene from one
        photograph. Its depth is estimated from a single picture, not measured
        from several, so there is no capture to build a mesh or terrain model
        from. Make the scene from a video or a set of photos to get one.
          
      
          
      
      
      
          
        
        
      
          
      
          
      
          

      
      
          
        Ground and terrain model
        
          
          
          Separates the ground from what stands on it, then grids
            it into a GeoTIFF — the file DL-TerrainSlicer turns into
            contours and laser-cut sheets.
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
            Stretch of the walk whole walk
            
          
              
              
            
          
          
          
          
          
          
          A developed
            strip, not a plan: true along the path and across it, deformed
            everywhere else. Do not measure areas off it.
          
          
          
            
            
          
          
          
          
        
          
      
          

      
          Two honest limits. The result is in COLMAP
        units until you calibrate against something of known size — the right
        shape, the wrong size. And a camera only reconstructs what it sees, so
        where planting hides the ground you get the top of the planting, not the
        terrain. Bare ground, paving, kerbs, walls and quarry faces are what this
        is for.
